Privacy Policy

TimeCoder is designed to do its work on your Mac. The app does not use advertising, analytics SDKs or developer-operated cloud services, and it does not sell personal data.

Data you choose to use

TimeCoder opens audio files and project files that you select. It analyses audio and stores timing points, labels, notes, settings and optional audio references or embedded audio in project and recovery files on your Mac. This content is not sent to the developer.

Local network access

If you choose to export directly to an ETC Eos console, TimeCoder connects to the host and port you provide on your local network. It exchanges show-control information needed to inspect an Event List, add timecode events and verify the result. This information goes directly between your Mac and the console, not through a developer server.

Storage and deletion

Project files remain where you save them. TimeCoder also keeps app settings and a local recovery copy so interrupted work can be restored. You can remove saved projects in Finder and remove local app data by deleting TimeCoder’s container from your Mac. Data sent to an Eos console remains in that show file until you remove it there.

TestFlight and Apple

When you install or test TimeCoder through TestFlight, Apple may process account, device, usage, crash and feedback information under Apple’s own policies. The developer may receive TestFlight crash reports, session metrics and feedback that Apple makes available to help improve the beta.
